Two Ways to Earn

Bring deals. Or bring people.

Both tracks are real, both are compensated, and both are structured properly — through your professional licence on the property side, and through the fund's licensed placement framework on the capital side.

Track 1 · Deal Flow — Open Now

Source the assets

The fund buys through a disciplined pipeline: advisory sources the deal, an underwriting dossier is prepared, the investment committee approves against defined criteria, an independent valuation confirms pricing, and the SPV closes. Your local knowledge and off-market access are exactly what feeds that pipeline.

  • Off-market and pre-market opportunities prioritised
  • Full underwriting support — you bring the asset, we do the dossier
  • Fast, criteria-driven decisions from a standing committee
  • You keep your client relationship on the sell side
Compensation
Sourcing fee per closed acquisition
Agreed per mandate, paid at SPV close alongside the fund's 1% advisory deal fee structure. Standard real-estate brokerage on the transaction remains yours where applicable.
Track 2 · Introductions — Via Licensed Agent

Introduce investors

The raise is led from Luxembourg with a placement agent licensed in each jurisdiction, on a 1–2% success-fee structure. Qualified introductions from professionals are welcomed — and compensated — through that licensed framework, in accordance with the rules of each jurisdiction.

  • Professional and well-informed investors only (€50K minimum ticket)
  • Introductions formalised through the licensed placement agent
  • Referral compensation defined in writing before any introduction
  • You never handle funds, paperwork, or investment advice
Compensation
Referral share of the placement success fee
Indicative — finalised with the licensed placement agent and Luxembourg counsel, per jurisdiction. Direct transaction-based compensation outside licensed channels is not offered.
What We Buy

The acquisition criteria.

Income-producing Portuguese real estate, held long, one SPV per asset. The pilot — a ~€11M, 1,000-hectare Alentejo estate — sets the tone: institutional scale, off-market, specialty assets with multiple income streams.

Lisbon & urban yield

Apartments and small buildings at ~3–5% net yield after management — the worked example in the memorandum is a €750K Lisbon apartment at 4.2% net.

Specialty estates

Hunting, agricultural, and hospitality-optional estates at institutional scale — single title, defensible income, aligned with a ten-year horizon.

Off-market first

Priority on off-market and pre-market access where a relationship — yours — is the gate. Speed and discretion on both sides.

The fine print, up front.

This page describes a partner programme, not a securities offering. Any investment in BBRF-PT is available exclusively to professional and well-informed investors by private placement, on the basis of definitive documentation. Compensation for capital introductions is structured exclusively through placement agents licensed in the relevant jurisdiction and formalised in writing before any introduction; the fund does not pay unlicensed intermediaries transaction-based compensation for the placement of securities. Property-sourcing compensation is paid for real-estate services under your professional licence. All terms are indicative pending Luxembourg and Portuguese counsel sign-off.
